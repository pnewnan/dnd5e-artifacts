Leshy of the Cronewood

"The trees are the face of the forest.
The roots are its bones. The mycelium is its memory."
← Back to Campaign Home
SizeMedium or Small
Speed30 ft.
TypeFey + Humanoid
LanguagesCommon, Sylvan
Darkvision60 ft.
GiftsLevels 1, 3, 5, 7

The Leshy are fey spirits bound to a particular Wood. Each Wood shapes its Leshy according to its own nature, creating guardians who embody the character, traditions, and mysteries of their homeland.

Your character is a Leshy of the Cronewood: an ancient, witch-haunted forest of crooked paths, ravens, wolves, old bargains, and mycelial memory. The Cronewood does not always remember as mortals do. It remembers patterns, trespasses, hungers, promises, blood, fire, grief, and return. Names matter less to it than what a creature has done within its bounds.

As a Leshy of the Cronewood, you carry a fragment of that ancient awareness wherever you travel.

Race Features
Creature Type

You are both Fey and Humanoid. You are affected by abilities targeting either type.

Fey Resilience

You have advantage on saving throws against being Charmed. Magic cannot put you to sleep.

Forest Sense

You always know which direction is north, the direction of the nearest substantial woodland within one mile, and whether you are within the bounds of a forested area. You cannot become naturally lost while traveling in forests.

Voice of Nature

You possess an innate connection to the hidden mycelial network that binds the natural world together. You may communicate simple concepts and emotions with beasts, plants, and fungi. This communication does not use language — information is exchanged through impressions, emotions, scents, memories, and instincts.

The information received is often incomplete or symbolic. A mushroom may communicate:

Fire Fear Stranger

Rather than: "A human carrying a torch passed here two hours ago."

Child of the Mycelium

When magic would allow you to communicate with plants or fungi, you may choose to speak with the collective network instead of a single organism. The network rarely provides precise answers — it communicates in patterns, impressions, emotions, and relationships.

Instead of "Sir Aldric entered the forest," you may hear: "The crown-bearer crossed the boundary."

Shapechanger

The Wood teaches that not all things need walk to be alive.

Once per long rest, you may transform into an inanimate natural object associated with your Wood for up to 1 hour.

· Tree stump
· Thorn bush
· Fallen log
· Patch of reeds
· Mushroom colony
· Dead tree
· Moss-covered boulder
· Tangled roots
· Dense shrub

While transformed:

  • You retain your game statistics and senses.
  • You are aware of your surroundings.
  • You cannot speak or cast spells with verbal components.
  • You cannot move.
  • You cannot take actions other than ending the transformation.

This feature ends early if you choose to end it.

Druid: Circle of Spores

Many Leshy become Druids, serving as protectors of the hidden cycles of life, death, decay, and renewal. A Cronewood Leshy's magic is expressed through fungi, roots, spores, moss, and the unseen networks beneath the forest floor.

Each feature below includes its mechanics and a Cronewood flavor note describing how it looks and feels for this character.

Circle Spells
2nd-level feature · Unlocks at Druid 3

Your symbiotic link to fungi and your ability to tap into the cycle of life and death grants you access to certain spells. At 3rd, 5th, 7th, and 9th level you gain access to the spells listed for that level. These spells are always prepared and do not count against the number of spells you can prepare each day.

Druid LevelSpells
3rdblindness/deafness, chill touch, gentle repose
5thanimate dead, gaseous form
7thblight, confusion
9thcloudkill, contagion
In the Cronewood, these spells manifest through the mycelial network. Blindness spreads like spore clouds. Gentle repose is the Wood preserving what it is not yet ready to reclaim. Animate dead is the network refusing to release what has fallen.
Halo of Spores
3rd-level feature

You are surrounded by invisible, necrotic spores that are harmless until you unleash them. When a creature you can see moves into a space within 10 feet of you or starts its turn there, you can use your reaction to deal necrotic damage unless it succeeds on a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC.

Druid LevelDamage
3rd–5th1d4 necrotic
6th–9th1d6 necrotic
10th–13th1d8 necrotic
14th+1d10 necrotic
For a Cronewood Leshy, these spores appear as glowing motes, drifting fungal threads, or tiny mushroom caps that bloom and burst. The Wood's memory releasing into the air.
Symbiotic Entity
3rd-level feature

As an action, you can expend a use of your Wild Shape to awaken your spores rather than transforming into a beast. You gain 4 temporary hit points per druid level. While active:

  • When you deal Halo of Spores damage, roll the damage die a second time and add it to the total.
  • Your melee weapon attacks deal an extra 1d6 necrotic damage.

These benefits last for 10 minutes, until you lose all the temporary hit points, or until you use Wild Shape again.

Rule: Temporary hit points don't stack. If you receive more while you still have some, you choose which to keep — you don't add them together.
The Cronewood briefly grows through you. Bark-like skin. Fungal growths along your arms. Glowing mycelial veins visible beneath the surface. You are no longer just a guardian — the Wood is wearing you.
Fungal Infestation
6th-level feature

Your spores gain the ability to infest a corpse and animate it. When a Small or Medium beast or humanoid dies within 10 feet of you, you can use your reaction to animate it. It stands immediately with 1 hit point, uses the zombie stat block, and remains animate for 1 hour before collapsing.

In combat, the zombie acts immediately after your turn. It obeys your mental commands and can only take the Attack action (one melee attack).

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum once). All uses regain on a long rest.

The network reclaims what has fallen. The dead briefly move because the Wood has not yet finished with them. It is not cruelty — it is the forest's refusal to waste what remains.
Spreading Spores & Fungal Body
10th and 14th-level features

Full mechanics to be added when relevant. At 10th level the mycelial network erupts from the soil around you. At 14th level your body becomes increasingly intertwined with the fungal network — you are no longer merely a guardian of the Cronewood. You are becoming part of it.

Roleplaying Notes

The Cronewood thinks differently than mortals. It remembers stories, emotions, relationships, and promises more readily than names, dates, or titles.

A Leshy often receives impressions rather than facts. A memory may reveal:

Fire.

Rather than: A hunter carrying a torch passed this way yesterday.

A Leshy's understanding of the world is shaped by connections rather than categories. Nothing is truly separate. Everything eventually returns to the Wood.

Age: Leshy mature at a rate similar to humanoids but may live for many centuries. Their connection to their Wood often makes it difficult to distinguish age from memory, and many Leshy measure their existence in seasons rather than years.
