# Leshy of the Cronewood — D&D Beyond Species Description

---

## Flavor Text

The Leshy are fey spirits bound to a particular Wood — ancient guardians who embody the character, traditions, and mysteries of their homeland. Each Wood shapes its Leshy differently. A Leshy of the Brightwood is a thing of light and birdsong. A Leshy of the Stonewood is slow, deep, and patient as granite. A Leshy of the Cronewood is something older and stranger.

The Cronewood is a witch-haunted forest of crooked paths, ravens, wolves, old bargains, and mycelial memory. It does not remember as mortals do. It remembers patterns, trespasses, hungers, promises, blood, fire, grief, and return. Names matter less to it than what a creature has done within its bounds.

As a Leshy of the Cronewood, you carry a fragment of that ancient awareness wherever you travel. You are a guardian, a messenger, a living root — and wherever you walk, the Wood is watching through you.

---

## Traits

**Creature Type.** You are both Fey and Humanoid. You are affected by abilities that target either type.

**Size.** Medium or Small (your choice at character creation).

**Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

**Languages.** You can speak, read, and write Common and Sylvan.

**Age.** Leshy mature at a rate similar to humanoids but may live for many centuries. Their connection to their Wood often makes it difficult to distinguish age from memory, and many Leshy measure their existence in seasons rather than years.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Constitution, Charisma, or Wisdom score increases by 1 (your choice).

---

**Wayward Path.** Once per long rest, as an action, choose a creature within 60 feet that you can see. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier) or become disoriented for 10 minutes. While disoriented, the creature has disadvantage on Survival checks made to navigate or track, cannot reliably determine direction, finds trails and landmarks unfamiliar or misleading, and cannot benefit from nonmagical effects that would prevent becoming lost. The effect ends early if the creature spends 1 minute carefully orienting itself and succeeds on a Wisdom (Survival) check against your save DC.

**Fey Resilience.** You have advantage on saving throws against being Charmed. Magic cannot put you to sleep.

**Forest Sense.** You always know which direction is north, the direction of the nearest substantial woodland within one mile, and whether you are within the bounds of a forested area. You cannot become naturally lost while traveling in forests.

**Voice of Nature.** You may communicate simple concepts and emotions with beasts, plants, and fungi. This communication does not use language — information is exchanged through impressions, emotions, scents, memories, and instincts. The information received is often incomplete or symbolic. A mushroom may convey *Fire. Fear. Stranger.* rather than "A human carrying a torch passed here two hours ago."

**Child of the Mycelium.** When a spell or ability would allow you to communicate with plants or fungi, you may choose to speak with the collective mycelial network rather than a single organism. The network communicates in patterns, impressions, emotions, and relationships rather than direct answers.

**Shapechanger.** Once per long rest, you may use an action to transform into an inanimate natural object associated with your Wood for up to 1 hour. You may take the form of a tree stump, fallen log, mushroom colony, moss-covered boulder, dense shrub, patch of reeds, dead tree, thorn bush, or tangled roots. While transformed, you retain your game statistics and senses. You are aware of your surroundings. You cannot speak, cast spells with verbal components, move, or take any action other than ending the transformation. The transformation ends early if you choose to end it.

---

**Cronewood Gifts.** At 1st, 3rd, 5th, and 7th level, you choose one Cronewood Gift from the list below. Some gifts have prerequisites.

---

### Gifts Available at Level 1

**Mycelial Keeper.** You gain proficiency in Nature. Additionally, once per long rest, as an action, you may touch soil, fungi, moss, roots, or rotting wood and listen to the mycelial network. The DM provides one sensory impression regarding a significant event that occurred nearby within the last seven days. The memory is emotional and symbolic rather than factual — you may receive impressions such as *Fear. Blood. Fire.* rather than a precise account.

**Witchwood Touched.** Your connection to the Cronewood has attuned you to the subtle magic that permeates the Wood. You gain proficiency in Arcana. Additionally, once per long rest, you may focus on a creature, object, or location within 30 feet to learn whether it is currently affected by magic. This ability reveals only the presence or absence of magic — not the spell, source, school, or specific effect.

**Rootspeaker.** Your connection to the Wood runs deeper than most Leshy. When using Voice of Nature to communicate with plants, roots, fungi, or moss, you receive clearer impressions than normal. Whenever the DM provides information through Voice of Nature, they provide one additional useful detail. The information still comes through symbols, emotions, and instincts rather than spoken language.

**Ravenbound.** You gain proficiency in Perception. You may communicate simple concepts with ravens and crows using Voice of Nature. Ravens and crows instinctively recognize you as part of the natural order of the Cronewood.

**Wolfkin Warden.** You gain proficiency in Survival and have advantage on checks made to track creatures through forests. Wolves recognize you as belonging to the Wood unless given a reason not to.

---

### Gifts Available at Level 3

**Pathkeeper.** You gain proficiency in Survival. If you are already proficient, your proficiency bonus is doubled for Survival checks. Additionally, once per long rest, you may spend 1 minute touching soil, roots, moss, fungi, natural stone, or cave silt to ask the DM one of the following questions: *Is there a hidden route nearby? Is there a safer route nearby? Has this area been traveled recently?* Answers come as impressions, not words. Worked stone generally blocks this connection.

**Deeper Into the Wood** *(Prerequisite: Wayward Path).* Your mastery of Wayward Path deepens. When you use Wayward Path, the duration increases to 1 hour. Additionally, the target has disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks made to identify trails, landmarks, or navigational clues, and disadvantage on saving throws against magical effects that would further disorient, mislead, or confuse them. The creature may repeat its saving throw every 10 minutes, ending the effect on a success.

**Voice of Beasts.** You may cast *Speak with Animals* once per long rest without expending a spell slot. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for this spell.

**Fungal Messenger.** Once per long rest, as an action, you may send a message of up to 25 words to a location within 1 mile that you have previously visited and that contains living plants, roots, fungi, moss, or soil. A creature within 10 feet of that location hears the message as a whisper.

---

### Gifts Available at Level 5

**Lost Roads** *(Prerequisite: Deeper Into the Wood).* When you use Wayward Path, the duration increases to 8 hours. Additionally, the target cannot benefit from nonmagical navigation, familiar routes seem strange and unfamiliar, and the target may unknowingly retrace its steps or follow an incorrect path when traveling through natural environments. The exact manifestations are determined by the DM and reflect the subtle supernatural influence of the Cronewood rather than direct mind control. The creature may repeat its saving throw at the end of each long rest, ending the effect on a success.

**Crow's Omen.** Once per long rest, when you fail an ability check, attack roll, or saving throw, you may reroll it. You must use the new result.

**Wolf's Warning.** While in a forest, cave, swamp, or other natural environment, you cannot be surprised unless you are incapacitated.

**Voice of the Network** *(Prerequisite: Mycelial Keeper).* When using Mycelial Memory, your connection reaches farther into the network's past. The memory may originate from events occurring up to one month ago, rather than only within the last seven days.

---

### Gifts Available at Level 7

**Fruiting Body.** Once per long rest, when you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you may instead drop to 1 hit point. When this happens, fungal blooms, moss, or pale mycelial threads briefly spread across your body as the Cronewood pulls you back from the edge.

**Witchwood Intuition.** Once per long rest, when you meet a creature for the first time, you may ask the DM one of the following questions: *Does this creature feel connected to danger? Does this creature feel connected to loss? Does this creature feel connected to the supernatural?* The answer comes as a symbolic impression rather than a direct fact.

**Speak with Plants.** You may cast *Speak with Plants* once per long rest without expending a spell slot. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for this spell.

**Warden's Authority.** Beasts with Intelligence 3 or lower begin one attitude category friendlier toward you, unless they have a specific reason to be hostile.
