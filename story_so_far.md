# Hands That Hold the Line — Story So Far
*Internal GM Reference · Sessions 1–8 · Through June 22, 2026 (in-game: 13 Amberfell, Year 313)*

---

## The Party

**Vanya** — The operative. Arrived in Hollowgate under a Gilded Veil contract: find gate scribe Eric Huwithakay and silence him before he reports further. She has been running two tracks simultaneously — the party's shared investigation (real) and her own hidden mission. Eric was already dead when the party found him. His satchel — and the private note inside — has not yet been recovered in play.

**Brynn** — A harpy concealing her nature. Entered Hollowgate hidden in a supply wagon. Accidentally revealed her wings in Session 3 (fell while lunging at a coyote). Formally revealed herself to the party as a harpy in Session 4. She carries a staff topped with a hollow bone-like sphere that reads constellations — she is searching for something in the stars. In Session 4 she covertly pocketed a small silver ring bearing a subtle Serenthian Crest from Eric's go-bag. The others did not notice.

**Aurelia** — Connected to Eric Huwithakay through their shared Sarynthian background. She was the first to access Eric's correspondence and files. She recognized the carnival figure Chaelynn (from her homeland) in Session 1 — Chaelynn actively avoided her and they have not spoken since. She identified the corpse in the well (Session 8) as an Espionage Agent of the Crown of Sarynthia.

**Hypha of Chronewood** — Joined in Session 6. A tall female leshy (8 feet, antler-like branches, moss and fungi, earth-toned robes, staff). Guardian of the Cronewood and connected to its mycelial network. Came searching for the stolen Heart Seed — a sacred rune-marked acorn vital for the Grove's next growth cycle. The kobolds sold it to an unknown buyer. Her mission remains unresolved.

**Robyn** — A kobold. Encountered in Session 7 — he had appraised and sold goods from the stolen wagons without realizing they were stolen. Vanya seized him and he became their reluctant guide through the Warren. He led the party to the well chamber where Eric's body was found. He is currently with the party. (Note: this is the same Robyn who features in the Heart Seed arc planning documents — his role as guide and his "bad trade" moment with Tamsin are still ahead.)

## What Has Happened — The Full Arc

### Phase One: Hollowgate (Sessions 1–5, April 12 – May 18, 2026)

#### Session 1 — April 12 — *Shadows Over Hollowgate*

Three strangers arrived in Hollowgate separately without knowing the others existed.

**Vanya** came first. She surveilled Hollowgate's guards: light armor, minimal weapons, inconsistent patrols, one guard asleep on duty. From the innkeeper at the Crooked Lantern she heard that Elkenquay was taxing all goods entering Fastingbreak to squeeze the border towns dry. Food convoys had been going missing for weeks. She observed coyotes in the fields — children weren't safe in the woods.

**Brynn** entered hidden in the back of a supply wagon and nested in hay at the Stable. Before she'd settled in, she overheard a Bruised, Furious Man describe an attack on his convoy: he was beaten, left for dead, all goods stolen. He later demanded action at the Mayor's House and at the tavern. No one was moving fast enough for him.

**Aurelia** followed the sound of a blacksmith's hammer and found a Traveling Carnival setting up nearby. At the carnival she saw a Troll Hauling Timber and a Tiefling in a Blue Coat Patterned With Stars entertaining children. She identified the ringmaster as **Chaelynn** — someone from her homeland — who recognized her and immediately avoided being seen by her.

---

#### Session 2 — April 18 — *Carnival Secrets and Star Paths*

The carnival opened. Brynn went, bumped into Aurelia near the tents, and Aurelia introduced her to **Magnus Eillin** (the ringleader, wearing a coat with stories woven into its pattern). Magnus mentioned supply issues for the show — convoys heading to Hollowgate were going missing — and said he needed to negotiate with the innkeeper and the mayor about food and drink.

Meanwhile Vanya equipped herself at the General Trading Post: a smaller trap, barbed wire spools, rations, and gloves (3gp 5sp 2cp). She introduced herself to Brynn and Aurelia and complimented Brynn's appearance. The three agreed to speak privately and went to the Mill/Warehouse near the Tree Line.

In the mill loft, Brynn revealed her bird-like features to the group for the first time. The others absorbed this without incident. Aurelia and Vanya then went to the Main Road Gatehouse, where the clerk **Jeffrey** reported that the scribe Eric Huwithakay had vanished two weeks ago — his belongings still at his home, marked C6 on the town map. Vanya pressed Jeffrey for information about coyote dens.

---

#### Session 3 — April 26 — *Coyote Confrontation at Hollowgate*

Vanya discussed her existing contract with a town contact, emphasizing she needed to show specific things at the tree line rather than the fields. Aurelia requested access to the Scribe's Office to find Eric's notes — she was granted entry but found a stairwell and another passage rather than the papers she sought. Notes she did find separately led her to feel relieved about Eric's character.

Vanya asked the guards for permission to take Jeffrey to the small tree line near the mill. The group went: Vanya, Brynn, Jeffrey, and Aurelia (after gathering food).

A coyote appeared. Brynn lunged at it instinctively and fell, partially revealing her wings. Vanya killed it on the second arrow. The group returned to the mill, set the coyote down, and Brynn proposed they share their reasons for being in Hollowgate. Aurelia agreed — working together would serve their mutual goals, including getting to the carnival that evening. The alliance formed.

*Vanya had already identified Aurelia as a potential suspect in Eric's disappearance.*

---

#### Session 4 — May 10 — *Coyote Secrets and Carnival Shadows*

Brynn formally revealed her identity as a harpy to the group. Vanya skinned the coyote and drained its blood. Vanya also seized papers from Aurelia's cloak containing notes about supply disruptions — these indicated that Eric had been **investigating** the disruptions, not causing them:
- Six wagons expected from Harrowfield never arrived
- A caravan from the Southern Coast was stalled
- An iron tool shipment was held at the Elkenquay Checkpoint

The group decided to sneak into Eric's House during the carnival (they had permission to enter at any time). Vanya inspected the door for traps, found none, but failed to open it. Brynn broke a window to enter.

Inside Eric's House they found:
- A burn tray with destroyed correspondence
- A **Date-Based Cipher Key** capable of decoding Eric's scribe notes
- Signs of a prior search — someone had been there before them
- A journal entry: Eric believed someone had entered his house while he was away
- A **Hidden Correspondence Slot** for sending messages back to Sarynthia
- A board with cross-referenced notes on convoy movements
- A **go-bag** (concealed, untouched — Eric never retrieved it before leaving)
- **Brynn covertly pocketed a small silver ring bearing a subtle Serenthian Crest from the go-bag. Aurelia did not notice.**

As they secured the house, Brynn spotted two carnival workers approaching and persuaded them inside. Vanya told the workers there was "a poisoned princess" inside to explain their presence. Magnus Eillin — present as one of the workers — shattered a window with his elbow and rolled out into the night. The remaining workers explained that the carnival couldn't hold its evening show without food, and the mayor had posted a reward for recovering stolen convoy contents. They returned to the Carnival Grounds.

---

#### Session 5 — May 18 — *Mysteries of Hollowgate Unveiled*

Aurelia rejoined Brynn and Vanya after rest. They concluded the carnival workers were not involved in Eric's disappearance. Eric's role: he blended in to report on local conditions rather than pursuing a specific mission. Only Aurelia and Brynn knew much about him; his enemies were unlikely to be operating locally.

Brynn linked the timing of convoy thefts to the carnival's schedule. The maps they'd reviewed pointed to a site in the forest north of town. No new convoy was expected that night.

Brynn repaired the broken window at Eric's House. Then they went to the inn-tavern looking for the convoy driver. Bartender **Terry** identified **McCoy** as a regular wagon runner staying upstairs with a young lady. Brynn rented a room nearby and went to question him. McCoy revealed:
- His entire wagon was stolen about ten miles west of town
- The attackers were **kobolds** — small creatures with spears
- He showed his bruising and accepted a magical berry from Brynn
- The road west had become dangerous; many wagons had been hit
- Hollowgate depended on food passing through it; central settlements were better supplied

The group decided to visit the Mayor's office to secure a bounty before pursuing the kobolds.

---

### Phase Two: Into the Forest (Sessions 6–8, May 31 – June 22, 2026)

#### Session 6 — May 31 — *Kobold Confrontation in Hollowgate*

Brynn delivered wine to the tavern staff and thanked them. She shared McCoy's information with Vanya and Aurelia and proposed they withhold knowledge of the kobolds from the Mayor — to secure the bounty for themselves.

At the Mayor's office (cluttered, Mayor scolding), Aurelia led the conversation. The Mayor confirmed ongoing convoy thefts and offered **400 gold** for the recovery of stolen wagons. He explained the food shortages, corrected Brynn about the timing of the latest theft. Brynn suspected he was withholding something but saw no malicious intent. Vanya urged immediate action. Brief interaction with Magnus and a dwarf at the carnival site — they agreed to assist.

The group reviewed their map, proposed a route into the woods, and set out.

In the forest, they encountered **Hypha of Chronewood** — eight feet tall, antler-like branches, moss and fungi, brown and green hair flowing with earth-toned robes, carrying a staff. She was searching for something stolen.

The kobolds had taken the **Heart Seed** from the Grove — a sacred rune-marked acorn vital for the next life cycle. Hypha revealed this and connected to the mycelial network for guidance on the kobolds' location. The party formed a loose alliance with her.

Moving stealthily through the woods, they spotted two wagons and a kobold guard in a clearing. Combat began when Brynn spotted a kobold inside one of the wagons. Vanya shot. Hypha killed a kobold with her thorn whip. Aurelia used a guiding bolt and dropped prone to attack under the wagon. The last surviving kobold surrendered. A second unarmed kobold then emerged from the front wagon, confused.

---

#### Session 7 — June 7 — *Kobold Bargains and Dark Secrets*

Brynn pinned the badly wounded kobold beside the wagons. A second kobold — **Robyn** — approached in surprise. He revealed that the chieftain had ordered him to unload boxes into a cave for the kobolds; he had appraised and sold the goods from the wagons without understanding they were stolen. Vanya seized Robyn and directed him toward the cave. Hypha threw the first kobold against a wall demanding the Heart Seed's location.

Inside the cave, Brynn found a **Partial Map** on a sleeping guard's table, showing the Warren layout and marked traps (including a Cheese Trap and a pit). Hypha magically enlarged a guard's lantern to illuminate the tunnels. The guard woke, revealed the chieftain was deeper in the Warren, and was intimidated by Hypha into cooperation.

They navigated the tunnels and their various traps. They encountered a beast in an alcove — the chieftain's pet. Robyn warned of dangers ahead.

They reached a larger chamber. **Kobold Chieftain Grubb**: adorned with jewelry, flanked by fourteen kobolds. Brynn demanded: "Why have you been stealing the convoys?" Grubb denied having the Heart Seed and proposed a trade deal — food and limited information about the missing cargo. He had a mostly eaten corpse brought in as evidence of the consequences of previous trade arrangements with outsiders.

The session ended with the party considering Grubb's offer.

---

#### Session 8 — June 22 — *Cavern Clash and Discovery*

Back in the Warren with tensions already high: Brynn spotted the Chieftain's Pet with three kobolds, plus six more kobolds further back. Vanya offered to return troublemakers and tried to negotiate. The kobolds produced the mostly eaten corpse again — this time clearly wearing **Elkenquay vestments**, alleged to be the messenger from whoever was paying them.

Hypha demanded the Heart Seed. The chieftain admitted he had **sold it** and offered to take them to the buyer. He demanded entertainment first — a contest of strength.

Hypha cast magic on the Chieftain's Pet, making it friendly toward the party.

Vanya escalated by shooting the chieftain with arrows. Chaos broke loose. Hypha killed a kobold with her thorn whip. Brynn cast Thunder Wave — a 15-foot cube of thunder that slammed into several kobolds and the guard Krob. The Chieftain charged Vanya, wrestled her, and overpowered her. Pinned and bleeding, Vanya asked: *"Are you entertained?"* The chieftain laughed, pulled out his spear, and chose to let her live: *"For that, I will let you live!"* He waved her off as chaos continued around him. The Chieftain's Pet turned on him.

**Robyn** called out — he had found something. The party followed him to a **Small Silty Chamber at the Bottom of a Well**.

In the chamber: a corpse in a blue tunic.

Aurelia examined the body and identified it: an **Espionage Agent of the Crown of Sarynthia**.

The party confirmed the agent was dead. They noticed a stable ladder leading up and out of the well.

**This is where the story currently stands.**

## What the Party Currently Knows

- Hollowgate has been suffering food shortages due to sustained kobold raids on supply convoys
- The kobolds were being **paid** to steal the convoys — they did not care about the food itself
- Payment came from someone connected to Elkenquay (the messenger's corpse wore Elkenquay vestments)
- The Heart Seed was stolen from the Grove and subsequently **sold by Chieftain Grubb** to an unknown buyer — it is not in the Warren
- Grubb offered to take them to the buyer (relationship now complicated after Vanya shot him)
- **Eric Huwithakay is dead** — body found at the bottom of a well in the kobold cave complex
- Aurelia confirmed him as an Espionage Agent of the Crown of Sarynthia
- There is a stable ladder leading out of the well

---

## What the Party Does NOT Know (GM Only)

- **Vanya's mission**: Contracted by the Gilded Veil to silence Eric before he could report further. He was already dead when found. The contract remains technically open.
- **Eric's satchel**: Contains a partial tunnel map, a note ("Kobolds. Someone is paying them."), and tucked in the inner fold — separate and hidden — "DM. The Briar Cup. Tell her the western road remained dry." The satchel has not been found or opened in play.
- **"DM" means Dunmore**: It's Eric's private notation for Dunmore, not a code. His contact there is Ilyra Marr at The Briar Cup. The phrase is a dead drop confirmation.
- **The Briar Cup / Ilyra Marr / Seren Vale / The Rusty Bin**: Eric's entire intelligence chain. None of it is known to the party.
- **Who paid the kobolds**: The Elkenquay vestment connection is visible to the party. The actual contractor (connected to the Concord's economic warfare against Fastingbreak) is unknown.
- **Brynn's Serenthian ring**: The small silver ring with a subtle Serenthian Crest pocketed from Eric's go-bag in Session 4. Aurelia hasn't noticed. Its significance is not yet established.
- **Chaelynn**: Why she avoided Aurelia is unexplained. Their history from Aurelia's homeland hasn't been explored.
- **The Heart Seed buyer**: Unknown. Grubb is the only lead to their location.
- **Robyn's future**: He sold the Heart Seed to Tamsin in Dunmore, believing it was a rare egg. He does not know what he sold or what it cost. His "bad trade" moment is ahead.
